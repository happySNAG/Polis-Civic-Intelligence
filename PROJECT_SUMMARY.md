## Resume version

**Polis — Civic Intelligence Platform**  
Designed and directed development of a resident-focused civic intelligence platform with provenance-first evidence models, freshness tracking, human review, deterministic publishing, structured civic-data normalization, issue timelines, private civic records, and extensive automated testing. Built synthetic-first and Iowa-first, with governance requirements established before live-data ingestion.

## LinkedIn version

Polis helps residents understand local government through trustworthy, source-grounded civic intelligence. It turns scattered agendas, minutes, budgets, ordinances, and public records into understandable issue timelines and practical civic actions while preserving evidence, provenance, freshness, uncertainty, privacy, and human review.
